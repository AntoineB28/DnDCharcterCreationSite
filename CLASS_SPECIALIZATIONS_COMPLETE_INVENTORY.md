# SUMMARY: LEVEL-SPECIFIC CHOICE POINTS BY PATTERN

## By Level
- **Level 1**: Sorcier (deity), Prêtre (deity), Mystique (philosophy), Rapacier (bird), Vampire (initial setup)
- **Level 2**: Druide (animal form), Vigilant (miracle)
- **Level 3**: MOST COMMON - Almost all classes have specialization choice
- **Level 4-5**: Various (Moine form, Fourbesang mutation, Artificier begins)
- **Level 6**: Druide (improved form), Artificier (augmentation), Sorcier (transformation), Barde (none), Bastion (resistances)
- **Level 7**: Guerrier (style de combat), Chasseur (none)
- **Level 8**: Astromancien, Nécromancien, Artificier (armor), Vigilant (none)
- **Level 9**: Mage, Chasseur (benefit), Sorcier (transformation)
- **Level 10**: Rogue improvement, Fourbesang (mutation), Artificier (implant), Bastion (none)
- **Level 12**: Mage, Prêtre, Nécromancien
- **Level 14**: Almost all classes (improvement/additional choice)
- **Level 16**: Guerrier (second style), Mystique (aspect), Artificier (none), Bastion (none)
- **Level 18**: Mage, Barde, Artificier, Bastion
- **Level 19**: Sorcier

## Special Systems
- **Mutation/Augmentation**: Fourbesang (genetic), Artificier (biomechanical), Sorcier (physical transformations)
- **Alignment/Philosophy**: Sorcier (dark god), Vampire (blood line), Prêtre/Vigilant (deity), Mystique (light/void)
- **Companion Selection**: Rapacier (bird), Chasseur (animal - level 3 only), Druide (animal form)
- **Repeating Poetry**: Guerrier (style), Moine (martial form/advanced form), Barde (improvements)
